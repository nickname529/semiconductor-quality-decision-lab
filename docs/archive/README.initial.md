# Semiconductor Quality Decision Lab

A joint semiconductor quality decision project connecting wafer-level defect analytics with customer and business risk scenarios.

## Core Question

How should semiconductor quality signals be translated into inspection and human-review decisions when model uncertainty, false negatives, false positives, inspection capacity, and customer impact must be considered together?
